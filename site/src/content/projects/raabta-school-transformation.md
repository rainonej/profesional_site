As Curriculum and Program Development Consultant for Voyage Educare Foundation's Raabta School Transformation Program, I conducted a needs assessment and designed a curriculum framework for training across 17 partner schools.

I created seven training sessions with post-assessments and supporting resources for 80 school leaders, 240 teachers, and 750 parents.
