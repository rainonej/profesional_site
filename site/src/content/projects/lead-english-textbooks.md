At LEAD, I worked as Curriculum Development Manager on Project North Star and conceptualized and created five English textbooks focused on student-friendliness and building rigor.

I edited and refined lesson plans and learning resources used across 300 partner schools, and designed audiovisual resources, print resources, and assessments supporting more than 20,000 teachers.

Project North Star was intended to expand LEAD's curriculum to 2,500 schools, with a stated goal of reaching one million students in India.
