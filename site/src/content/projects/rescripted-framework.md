I worked with Re|Scripted as an independent research and curriculum contractor, conducting interdisciplinary research across girlhood studies, feminist theory, gender and education, and critical curriculum studies. The work supported the development of the curricular philosophy and rationale for a national girls' education nonprofit.

I synthesized scholarly articles, books, and other academic writing into an accessible research reference resource aligned with the curriculum.

I contributed research and writing to the curriculum rationale, including authoring a section grounded in relevant theoretical and empirical scholarship, and reviewed drafts with research-based revisions and recommendations to strengthen clarity and coherence.
