As an Arthur Zankel Fellow with Teachers College's Digital Futures Institute, I co-designed and implemented _Media Justice for Social Change_ for 100 fifth-grade students across three ICT classrooms at PS 187.

I authored the curricular rationale and introduced a coherent learning progression for an interdisciplinary fellowship team, aligning activities around explicit learning goals, scaffolded inquiry, and exploration of social justice through media.

I co-taught a fifth-grade cohort using zines and other media, and helped lead a Teachers College field experience where students used the DFI Media Studios to create podcasts, short films, and art about their ideas of community and identity.
